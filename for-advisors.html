Advisor Pro Tips

Get More From Every Session

🎯
Let Students Set Their Own Level

The skill level selector (Struggling / On Level / Advanced) is per-subject. A student who's advanced in History might be struggling in Algebra. Let them be honest — the AI adjusts accordingly.

🔁
Use "Try a Different Angle"

The generator has a regenerate button that cycles through four prompt angles: Standard, Analogy-First, Misconception-First, and Step-by-Step. If the first response doesn't click, one tap gets a different approach.

💾
Download Prompts to Keep

Students on free AI plans have daily message limits. Have them click "Download .txt" to save their best prompts — they can reuse them next session without burning through their quota.

📝
Use the Context Box

Tell students to describe what their teacher emphasized, what chapter they're on, or when their test is. This optional field sharpens the prompt dramatically — "Chapter 6, focusing on word problems, test Friday" vs nothing.

🤝
Pair Students Up

Have a strong student and a struggling student share a device and build the prompt together. The stronger student explains their choices — teaching a concept reinforces it as much as studying it alone.

📱
Works on Any Device

No app install. Students can use a school Chromebook, a personal phone, a tablet — anything with a browser. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot all have free mobile apps. No subscriptions needed.

A Real Generated Prompt

Here's an actual prompt the tool generates — this is what your student copies and pastes into any free AI chatbot. Notice how it's fully structured, grade-calibrated, and immediately usable.

10th Grade On Level Math Linear Equations Exam Review 
You are an expert, encouraging tutor for a 10th Grade high school student.
Skill level: On Level — Use standard grade-level language and a normal pace.

Subject: Math
Topic: Linear Equations
Task: Exam Review
Additional context from student: We just finished Chapter 6. My teacher says word problems will be on the test.

Create a test-prep review for "Linear Equations" for a 10th Grade student (On Level).
Include:
• 3 most likely question types on a test
• 4 facts/formulas/dates to memorize (bullets)
• 2 common traps to avoid
• 1 last-minute study tip
Total under 300 words.

Core rules:
• Stay focused only on the topic above — no tangents
• Match language complexity to the On Level level
• Be warm, direct, and motivating — never condescending
• Use clean formatting with clear labels
• Do not add unrequested sections
